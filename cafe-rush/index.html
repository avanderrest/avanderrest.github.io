Opening time

Customers queue at the counter with an order in their speech bubble. Make each item at the right appliance, then put it on the counter. Matching items are taken straight away.

  • Move with WASD or the arrow keys.
  • Use an appliance or the counter with E or Space.
  • ☕ Espresso from the espresso machines, 🍪 cookies and 🍫 brownies from the ovens.
  • Later machines finish something you bring them: espresso → milk bar → 🥛 latte → ice well → 🧋 iced latte, and 🥖 roll → sandwich press → 🥪 toastie.
  • New kit keeps arriving as far as day 17: a muffin oven, an ice well, a soup kettle, a bread oven, a sandwich press and a blender.
  • Drag the machines wherever you like between shifts.
  • You can carry one thing at a time. Spare items can wait on the counter or go in the bin.
  • Each day lasts 90 seconds. Faster service means bigger tips. Three walkouts and the day ends early.
  • Earnings go in the bank. Spend them on upgrades between days; every day gets busier.
