Marble Run

Level 1

  • Into the cup
  • Ring every bell
  • Par

The box

Pick one, then a cell

How to play

Build a run down the pegboard so the marble rolls from the hopper at the top into the brass cup.

  • Pick a piece from the box, then tap an empty cell to hang it there. The box only holds so many of each.
  • Tap a piece you have hung to choose it: Flip turns it round, Take back puts it back in the box. You can also drag it to another cell, or off the board to take it back. With a mouse, a right-click takes it back.
  • Release lets the marble go. If it misses the cup, touches the pipes round the level or comes to a stop, the board resets and your pieces stay where they are, so change one and try again.
  • Slopes come in two halves of one gentle line: a high slope across the top half of its cell and a low slope across the bottom half. High then low makes a smooth rail; the next high slope goes one row down. They come from the same pile.
  • The dark bronze pieces and the crates are part of the level and cannot be moved. Nothing can hang where there are no peg holes.

The pieces

    The sandbox

    • The Sandbox, under the levels, has no goal: a big board and every piece without limit, to build whatever you like.
    • It has pieces of its own: wheels that spin or turn by themselves, see-saws, a spiral, flippers, a catapult, a flap you set one way or the other, crumbling ledges, Plinko pins, boosters, bumpers, chimes that play a note (higher up, higher note) and buckets that catch and count. They are all in the list above.
    • Marbles come from a marble release you hang yourself, in any cell at the edge of the board; a starter slope under it catches each marble and sets it rolling. With more than one release, they take turns.
    • Release keeps the marbles coming until you press Stop, and you can hang, move, flip and take back pieces while they run.

    Stars

    • One for getting the marble into the cup, one for ringing every bell on the way (the marble only has to touch a bell's cell), and one for using no more pieces than par. They needn't all come in the same run.
    • On levels with two marbles, each cup takes one, and both have to end up home.
    • Hint shows one piece of a run that gets all three stars.

    Keys

    • Space releases or stops, F flips the chosen piece, Delete takes it back, H is a hint, Esc lets go of what you are holding.
    • Sound is off until you turn it on.
