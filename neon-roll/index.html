Neon Roll

Hold anywhere, or Space, to roll

How to play

One button. Hold the mouse, a finger, Space or ↓; let go to let go.

  • Hold on the way down. Holding presses the ball into the track, so every drop gives it more speed.
  • Let go before the rise. Holding on an upslope drags the speed back out of it. Released, the ball follows the hill up and flies off its crest.
  • Watch the ring. In the air, the ring shows where you'll come down if you let go now, and the small diamond where you'll come down if you hold: green is a Perfect, amber a near miss, red a slam. Hold to dive and the ring slides back towards the diamond; let go when it's green.
  • Land along the slope. Touch down at the same angle as the track for a Perfect. Hold in the air to dive onto a slope; come down against it and you slam and lose most of your speed. A near miss costs a little.
  • Flow. The ball always rolls on. Every Perfect in a row adds a level of flow: you cruise faster and fly higher off every crest. A near miss costs a little speed and keeps your flow; a slam drops you back to plain cruising.
  • Two Perfects in a row and the ball glides: it falls gently while you're not holding, so you can float out further and time your dive onto the next downslope. A landing short of Perfect ends it.
  • Three Perfects in a row set the ball on fire: a burst of speed and double shards.
  • P pauses. Restart any time with R or ↻. If you stop getting anywhere, the game offers it.
  • Gaps (in their own mode). Steep kickers throw you over breaks in the tube. Don't hold over a gap — diving there drops you short.

Modes

  • Endless — the blackout rolls in behind you and keeps getting faster.
  • Air Time — two minutes; your score is how long you spend in the air.
  • Sprint — the same 1500 m every time. Race your best.
  • Gaps — kickers over breaks in the tube. Falling in ends the run.

Shards you pick up are kept between runs and buy new balls.
