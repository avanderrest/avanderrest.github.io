Furrow

A bend in the river, four people and a handcart, and nothing else. Lay it all out: a barn for the stores, houses to sleep in, a field and a woodcutter — then a bakery, hens, sheep, a smithy, a tavern, a barber even, once there are scissors to be had. Everything you place, they build.

And then go and live there. Click anyone, and take their place for the day: breakfast, a morning’s work, an errand, supper, bed. Nobody makes you — but every task done pays the village, and renown is what opens up the better buildings.

How to play

Building

  • Pick a building from the bar along the bottom and click where it goes. The green square is its door: that needs open ground. It costs coin and logs up front, then goes up as a site that anyone out of work will come and hammer at.
  • Drag the map to look round (or WASD); the wheel or +/− zooms. Space pauses, 1–3 set the speed. Right-click or Esc puts the tool down.
  • Better buildings need renown ★. You get it for every building finished, every newcomer who settles, and every task done while living as somebody.
  • You start with nothing built. The villagers camp by their handcart on the green, eat from it and sleep by its fire; the first barn takes over the stores and the camp is packed away. Houses give them beds.
  • Nobody has a job until you give them one: click them and choose from the list, or click a workplace and hire. A spare bed and a contented village bring someone new down the road each morning.

How the village lives

  • Up at six, breakfast, work till noon, lunch, work till six, supper, the tavern, bed. They eat from the barn, so keep it stocked; hungry, homeless or idle people get unhappy, and the unhappy leave.
  • Farmers sow, water every day (from a well, or the river) and harvest. A trader sells what the village can spare for coin. The rest is chains: wheat → bread, logs → tools, sheep → wool → clothes.
  • The barber needs a blacksmith for the scissors. The clothes shop needs a sheep pen for the wool.
  • Anyone out of work for long may start lifting purses. Three lifts and they are a thief. A job puts them straight.

Living as someone

  • Click anyone to follow them, then Live as… (or L). WASD or the arrows walk, Shift runs, or click where to go. Esc steps back and they carry on without you.
  • E does what the prompt at the bottom says: eat at home, work your job, fill a can, fell a tree, talk to someone. R does the second thing on offer.
  • Today’s list is top right. Meals, your work, an errand and bed by eleven: each one done pays coin to the village and renown. All of them in a day pays a bonus.
  • Out of work? Stand at the door of a workplace with an opening and ask for the job — or come up behind someone and press Q to lift their purse. Anyone facing you sees it.
  • Walk into the barber or the clothes shop to change how you look. It comes out of your own purse.
  • Go to bed at home after half seven and the night passes. Stay up past two and you drop where you stand.

The village saves itself on this device every morning and every half minute.
