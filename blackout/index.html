Blackout

How to play

You are outside a compound at night with a pistol and a pair of night-vision goggles. Get in, take the data off the terminal, and be back at the fence before they find you.

Pick normal or hard on the title screen with ← →. Normal gives you fifteen rounds, enough to put out every lamp you need to. Hard gives you five, and every one is a decision.

You see clearly only a short way around you. Past that is dark, and all that reaches you is light — lamps, torches, cameras — blurred until it comes close.

Every control is on the keyboard, and on the buttons along the bottom of the screen.

Moving

  • ← → — sneak. This is silent.
  • Shift — run. Fast, and guards hear it from a long way off.
  • ↓ — crouch. Slow, silent, and the only way through a vent.
  • Each footstep shows as small ( ) marks at your feet: one pair for a walk, three for a run. They turn amber when a guard heard that step.
  • ↑ — climb a ladder, or step up onto a crate.

Staying unseen

  • The light meter top-left fills as you step into lamplight. Below a quarter you are invisible past a couple of paces — guards walk straight by.
  • A torch beam that touches you fills the guard’s ? marker. Let it fill and it turns to ! and the compound wakes up.
  • A torch is light too: close up, it finds you even in the dark. Crouch behind a crate and the crate takes the beam.
  • X fires the silenced pistol at whatever is in front of you. A lamp bursts and leaves a pool of darkness you can work in. So does a camera.
  • It will drop a man too — but only one whose back is turned and who has not noticed you. Fire at a guard who is facing you and all you do is tell him where you are. The pistol is for the lamps.
  • When something new comes into your line of fire, a faint red laser swings onto it for a moment to show what the next round will hit. A man in front of you is picked over a lamp overhead, and you can only shoot what you can see clearly. On a guard the dot is amber when the shot would not be a quiet one.
  • C drops the goggles. You see in the dark; the battery does not last.

Guards

  • Walk up behind an unsuspecting guard and press Z to choke him out.
  • A body lying in the light is as good as an alarm. Hold Z to drag it somewhere dark.
  • One of them carries a keycard. You need it. Step over him once he is down.

The job

  1. Get inside the wire — there is a vent at the foot of the wall.
  2. Take the keycard, open the service door.
  3. Upstairs to the terminal. Z to hack: stop the marker in the green, three times.
  4. The moment the download lands, every alarm in the place goes off, and the fence you came in by shows green through the dark. Get back to it — the job is not done until you do.

A missed key on the terminal costs nothing but a noise; try again. And once the data is yours, the terminal is a checkpoint: die on the way out and Z puts you back there.

Two bullets will kill you. P pauses. Progress is kept on this device only.
