Blackout

Nothing moves until you do. Move, or do anything, and the guards take their steps; stand still and the whole compound waits. Their torches are cones you can read, and the dark is the only friend you have.

Hard gives you far fewer bullets, and the guards notice you quicker.

Terminal — bypass

> KEY 1 / 3

Press Z, Space or the button while the marker is in the green. Three keys. A miss only makes a noise. Esc steps away.

Out

How to play

Get into the compound, take the data off the terminal on the building's upper floor, and get back out through the wire you came in by. There are five missions, each a different compound and each harder than the last; winning one opens the next, and any you have reached can be picked from the title.

Moving

  • There are no turns. Nothing moves until you do: for every two moves you make — a step, a shot, a takedown, opening a door — each guard takes one step. Once the alarm is up they step with every move. Stand still and the compound waits.
  • Click anywhere to walk there, or step with the arrow keys / WASD. The map is isometric, so each key steps along the grid: ↑ up-left, → up-right, ↓ down-right and ← down-left. A key cap on the floor shows where each press goes. Hold a key to keep walking.
  • Space stays put for one move: you stand still and the guards carry on, so you can wait for a patrol to pass without walking in circles. Click somewhere else, or press a key, to change course mid-walk; Esc stops a walk.
  • The blue tiles around you are your move grid. A tile tinted red is one a guard or camera can see right now.
  • R runs: a running step counts as half a move, so you cover twice the ground, but every step is heard by any guard close by.
  • Stairs are tiles like any other: walk onto them to change floor.

Light and being seen

  • A guard's torch is the yellow cone. Close up, the torch finds you even in the dark. Further out, the guard only sees you if you are standing in lamplight. The Light square shows how lit your tile is.
  • Each time someone spots you the Seen bar jumps and you stop where you are. The guard leaves his beat to look where he saw you — a ? marks the spot — one step at a time. If you are gone when he gets there, he looks around and goes back to his route. The bar drains while nobody sees you; fill it and the compound wakes up.
  • You only see clearly a short way into the dark. Past that is fog, and all that shows through it is light — lamp pools and torch cones, with nobody visible behind them.
  • C puts the goggles on: you see much further in the dark, while the battery lasts.

Guns and hands

  • Click a lamp or camera (or Tab to pick, X to fire) to shoot it out. One round. Breaking glass is heard close by.
  • The pistol kills a guard outright. But a dead man who is found raises the alarm.
  • Walk up beside or behind a guard and click him / press Z to knock him out. If another guard finds him, he walks over and wakes him up — no alarm, but now there are two of them looking.
  • A shot, a takedown or a door counts as a move, just like a step.
  • A dead body that a guard or camera sees is an alarm. Z on or next to a body picks it up; Z again puts it down. Dragging is slow: each step counts as two moves.

The job

  1. Get inside the wire — there is a cut in it by where you start.
  2. One of the patrol carries a keycard. Take him down and step over him.
  3. The card opens the service door. The terminal is upstairs. Z next to it to hack: stop the marker in the green three times.
  4. The download trips every alarm in the place. Get back to the cut in the wire. The terminal is a checkpoint from then on.

Drag the map to look around, scroll or use − / + to zoom, F recentres. Two bullets will kill you. Best runs are kept for each mission, on this device only.
