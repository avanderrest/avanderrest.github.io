Tithe

How to play

You are Wren, a thief of the Lowmarket. Three people in Vell got rich on the famine. Climb each of their houses, take back what they took, and at the top decide what to do with them.

Climbing

  • ← → run. Space jumps — hold it for a full jump, tap it for a hop, and steer in the air.
  • Your hands catch any cornice or crack they pass on the way down. Walk off the end of a cornice and you turn and catch its edge. Hold ↓ to let ledges go by.
  • ↑ jumps straight up for a ledge overhead, climbs ivy, and pulls you up onto a cornice you are hanging from. You can jump up through a cornice from below.
  • ↓ on a cornice lowers you to hang from its edge. Hanging, ← → shimmy, ↑ ↓ go hand over hand up or down a column of cracks, and ↓ with nothing below lets go.
  • Hanging, Space with a direction lunges across a missing stone.
  • Four storeys is a hard landing. Nine is the last one.

Guards

  • A guard sees along his own ledge — the pale strip in front of him. The ? fills while he makes you out. When it turns to ! he comes for you.
  • Hang below his ledge and he walks straight over you. Step behind a hanging banner and he walks past.
  • Behind him: E chokes him out. He sleeps till dawn.
  • Wren doesn't kill unless you choose to let her. There are knife racks in the kitchens and the watch house, and the fence sells one. Take a knife and Q kills from behind, and hanging below a guard's feet Q pulls him off the ledge.
  • A guard who finds a body raises the alarm, and every guard sees a little further after that.

Windows

  • Stand at a lit window and press ↑ to climb in. Shuttered windows need a lockpick.
  • Inside, the arrows walk you about the room — ↑ toward the back wall, ↓ toward you. Whatever is within reach gets its name and a marker over it; E uses it: search, hide, snuff, pull. Esc (Leave) walks you back out of the window.
  • You arrive with just your head over the sill, out of sight. Take a look: an arrow climbs in, ↓ drops you back outside.
  • Whoever is in the room sees across the floor the way they face — the pale cone. If they get a good look at you they say ? and come over to search, looking both ways. Keep out of sight and they give up. A second good look while they're searching, and they raise the house.
  • Caught, you drop back onto the sill and the room settles again. No heart lost, but they're warier, and the alarm counts against you.
  • To hide, step up to a wardrobe, a curtain or a cupboard and press ↑. Any other arrow steps you back out.
  • Search while their back is turned, hide in a wardrobe or behind a curtain, pinch out the candle so they see half as far, and in a kitchen tug a wire on the servants' bell board: the cook thinks she is wanted upstairs and goes to answer it.
  • 1 takes out a pebble, 2 a sleep dart. The arrows then step through what you could aim at, E lets fly, Esc puts it away. A pebble is a distraction: whoever hears it land walks over to look, turning their back on you. While you aim, a flashing ? marks everyone who will hear it. A dart puts someone to sleep from a distance.
  • Get close behind someone who hasn't seen you: E knocks them out. With a knife, Q kills — but servants and clerks are innocents, and the city remembers.

The end of a contract

At the top of each house is the person the silver was taken for, in a room of their own. How you deal with them depends on what you find on the way up: a note about a rotten bell or a nightly glass of port, the proof that would ruin them, the tool the job needs. Each way you find goes into the quest log (top right, L to fold it away) with what it still needs, so you know your choices before you go in. In their room, do it while their back is turned — or climb back out and leave them be. Then you decide who gets the silver: the Lowmarket, or you. Both choices follow you to the end.

A chalked sparrow on the wall is a checkpoint. Progress is kept on this device only.
