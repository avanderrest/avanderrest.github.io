    The desk

    • Lift off to slow down. A toy car coasts badly, so letting go of the throttle sheds speed quickly — you rarely need the brake for a bend.
    • Clear tubing has walls you can lean on, and grips better than anything else on the desk.
    • The steel rule is quick and horribly slippery. Straighten up before you touch it.
    • The floppy stack is a jump. Land pointing the way you are travelling or it scrubs your speed off.
    • Spilt coffee takes your grip away for as long as your tyres stay wet.
    • Bare wood is slow going, and the clutter on it does not move.
    • Tuck in behind a rival to fill the slipstream bar, then cash it in with Shift.
    • There is room for two cars side by side everywhere, so go round rather than through.
