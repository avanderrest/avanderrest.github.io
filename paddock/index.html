    How to play

    Garage. Pick an engine, gearbox, tyres, body, suspension and one extra. Watch the bars: top speed and acceleration win straights, handling and grip win corners, off-road decides how much you suffer when you leave the tarmac.

    Tracks. Choose one of the built-in circuits or draw your own. Set laps and how many rivals turn up.

    Race. Arrow keys or WASD. Up to go, down to brake or reverse, left and right to steer. Shift or Space fires nitro if you fitted a bottle. Yellow pads on the road are boosts.

    Editor. Paint road, gravel, mud, boost pads, trees and walls. Place a start line on the road; the road must loop back round to it. Save it and it appears in your track list.
