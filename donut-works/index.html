How to play

  • Dough goes in one end, money comes out the other. A Hopper plops out dough, a Ring Press punches the hole, a Fryer cooks it, and a Shop Counter sells whatever reaches it. Join them up with belts.
  • Pick something in the Build tab, then click a tile to place it. Press R to turn it before you place it. With the belt tool, click and drag to paint a whole run of belts at once, and they will follow your hand.
  • Right-click anything to take it back for a full refund. Click a machine to open it in the bench under the floor: change its flavour, tune it up, turn it or remove it.
  • Machines take things in from the back or sides and push them out the front (the little arrow). Things stuck at the end of a belt with a ! have nowhere to go.
  • Belts running into the side of another belt merge onto it. A Splitter does the opposite, dealing one line out left, ahead and right, which is how you feed two slow fryers from one press, or send half the donuts off to be pink.
  • Glazers, Toppers and Fillers only work on fried donuts. Fry a plain dough ball and you get a blob. Fry a donut twice and you get charcoal. People will buy charcoal, but they will not be happy.
  • Certain combinations are named recipes and pay half again as much. Find them in the recipe book. Some of them are sensible. The Special on the board pays double while it lasts.
  • Each level is an order from the shop out front. Fill it to unlock new machines, toppings and a cash bonus. Your factory stays as it is between levels, so build on what you have.
