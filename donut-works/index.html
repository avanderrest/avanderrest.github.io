How to play

  • Dough goes in one end, money comes out the other. A Mixer plops out dough, a Ring Press punches the hole, a Fryer cooks it, and a Shop Counter sells whatever reaches it. Join them up with belts.
  • Pick something in the Build tab, then click a tile to place it. Press R to turn it before you place it. With the belt tool, click and drag to paint a whole run of belts at once, and they will follow your hand.
  • Right-click anything to take it back for a partial refund. Click a machine to open it in the bench under the floor: change its flavour, tune it up, turn it or remove it. Drag anything on the floor to move it somewhere else — drop it on something and the two swap places, so you can shuffle a line about without pulling it up. Placing a new thing on an occupied tile replaces what is there, refunded at the take-back rate.
  • Machines take things in from the back or sides and push them out the front (the little arrow). The tag under a machine says what it is set to, and whatever it is working on rides in the middle of it.
  • A line backing up is normal: the fryer is slow, so dough queues behind it. That is a full line, not a broken one. A red ! means something genuinely has nowhere to go — hover it and the floor will tell you why.
  • Belts running into the side of another belt merge onto it, and a belt fed only from its side bends round to take the corner. A Splitter, yours from the start, deals one line out left, ahead and right, which is how you feed two slow fryers from one press, or send half the donuts off to be pink.
  • A Joiner is the splitter backwards: three ways in, one way out, and it takes turns between them so a busy line cannot hog the belt. It is the tidy way to bring several lines back to the shop front.
  • The Shop Counter is the shop front, and a shop front is one bank of tills: you can have as many counters as you like, but each new one has to go next to another counter.
  • Glazers, Toppers and Fillers only work on fried donuts. Fry a plain dough ball and you get a blob. Fry a donut twice and you get charcoal. People will buy charcoal, but they will not be happy.
  • Certain combinations are named recipes and pay half again as much. Hover any entry in the recipe book — even a ? ? ? one you have not made yet — and it will spell out the glaze, filling and toppings it wants. Some of them are sensible. The Special on the board pays double while it lasts.
  • Each level is an order from the shop out front, and the Orders tab is the list of what it still wants, with the exact recipe under each line. The Level box in the header shows the same thing on hover, and clicking it opens the tab. Fill the order to unlock new machines, toppings and a cash bonus. Your factory stays as it is between levels, so build on what you have.
