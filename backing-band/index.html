Pick a song

How to play

  1. Listen to the band. Every instrument plays together, and each button lights up as it is pressed.
  2. Pick one instrument and press Show me to hear just that part on its own.
  3. Press My turn and play the part back, note by note. Notes that sound together can go in either order.
  4. A wrong note flashes red and the right one glows, so you can carry on. Your score is the share of beats you got right first time; 100% completes the part, and the song shows how complete it is overall.
  5. A button that stays half-lit with a bar running down is a long note: it is still one tap, and it rings on by itself.
  6. Longer songs come in sections — intro, verse, chorus. Learn each one, then play the whole song.
  7. The singer sings along when you listen — turn Vocals off for just the band. You never have to play the singer.
  8. Timeline mode is the other way to learn a part: the bottom of the screen becomes a timeline of the whole song: drag the buttons onto it, then Play mine. Drag a placed note to move it, and drag along the section names to select a stretch you can copy and paste somewhere else. A note rings on until your next one, so a long note is just one note. Each note turns green when it's spot on, yellow when it's nearly there, orange for the right moment but the wrong note, and red when it's off. Each check scores what you got spot on, shown on the instrument tabs and the section names.
  9. Too fast? Drop the Speed to ½ or ¾ to practise.
  10. Mega Jam has every sound in the band — including horns, whistle, a shouted “Hey!” and effects that no song uses yet. Press Record, play, then Stop to make a loop — record again to layer on top.

Keys 1–9 play the buttons of the instrument you're on. You can tap any button at any time just to jam.
