Pick a song

How to play

  1. Listen to the band. Every instrument plays together, and each button lights up as it is pressed.
  2. Every instrument in the section is on screen at once. Press This section to hear it, watching the buttons light.
  3. Press My turn and play the part back, note by note. Notes that sound together can go in either order.
  4. A wrong note flashes red and the right one glows, so you can carry on. Your score is the share of beats you got right first time; 100% completes the part, and the song shows how complete it is overall.
  5. A button that stays half-lit with a bar running down is a long note: it is still one tap, and it rings on by itself.
  6. Longer songs come in sections — intro, verse, chorus. Learn each one, then play the whole song.
  7. The singer sings along when you listen — turn Vocals off for just the band. You never have to play the singer.
  8. The timeline under the band is the other way to learn a part: it shows the section you pick, every instrument in it. Drag a pad from the left onto it (or tap a slot), then Play mine. Drag a placed note to move it; tap it to remove it. A note rings on until your next one, so a long note is just one note. Each note turns green when it's spot on, yellow when it's nearly there, orange for the right moment but the wrong note, and red when it's off. The timeline has its own score, shown beside each instrument; only My turn completes a part.
  9. Too fast? Drop the Speed to ½ or ¾ to practise.
  10. Mega Jam is a drum machine with every sound in the band. Sixteen pads (keys 1–4, Q–R, A–F, Z–V); Edit pads puts any sound on any pad. The sequencer has a row per pad: tap a step to switch it, or press Record and play the pads in time to write them in. Pick a Groove to start from, and turn the knobs to shape the whole jam. Only rows with steps show: + Add a row brings one back, and tapping a row’s name twice removes it. The address bar always holds your mix, so to share it, just share the link.
  11. Band / Scope in the corner of the stage swaps the band for an oscilloscope: the sound itself, drawn as a wave.

Keys 1–9 play the buttons of the instrument you're on. You can tap any button at any time just to jam.
